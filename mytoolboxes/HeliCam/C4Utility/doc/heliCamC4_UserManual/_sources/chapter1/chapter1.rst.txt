Introducing the heliCam\ :sup:`TM` C4 Lock-In Camera
====================================================

The heliCam\ :sup:`TM` C4 lock-in camera enables rapid and massively
parallel dual-phase demodulation of optical signals. The centerpiece of
the camera is a custom-designed CMOS image sensor, the
heliSens\ :sup:`TM` S4. It has analogue signal processing circuitry
integrated into each pixel sensor unit cell; an approach known as *smart
pixel* technology. This way, the sensor addresses the two major
constraints of conventional image sensors when used for lock-in applications:
frame rate limitations and insufficient dynamic range. The former curbs
the maximum demodulation frequency and the latter hampers the
acquisition of signals with large offset but small modulation amplitude.
