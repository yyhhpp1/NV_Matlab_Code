Event And Status Diagram

The gen4 devices contain three sequence control loops running in parallel:

  • Acquisition: Main Loop. Controls the measurement sequence and synchronizes the other control loops. State information is available through the AcquisitionStatus features.

  • Transfer: Controls the transmission of the measurement data. State information is available through the TransferStatus features.

  • Stage: Controls the motion. State information is available through the StageStatus features.

The loops can be monitored and controlled through features (Software) or digital I/Os (Hardware).
