Get the installed Stage Type used and controlled by heliService

  • Unknown: Motion Type is unknown / not configured.

  • Disabled: Motion is not available on this device.

  • UltraEncoderType2: Motion with high precision encoder is available.

  • StandardEncoderType1: Motion with standard encoder is available.

  • UltraXenax: Motion controlled by external Xenax Controller with 100nm high precision encoder.

  • StandardXenax: Motion controlled by external Xenax Controller with 1um encoder.

  • StandardEncoderType4: Motion with standard encoder is available.

  • StandardEncoderType3: Motion with standard encoder is available.

  • Custom: Custom motion control is used.
